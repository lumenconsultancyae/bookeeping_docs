# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Lumen Accounting Suite

## Purpose & Product Scope

Lumen Accounting Suite provides UAE small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with a unified, browser-accessible bookkeeping, commercial operations, payroll, and tax compliance application. This PRD defines the current short-term product deliverable scope separately from the target architecture delta documented in the companion Software Design Document (SDD).

---

## Target Personas

- **Chief Executive Officer / Founder**: Monitors Cash Runway, 90-day Monthly Burn, Net Profit Margin, 6-month Cash Flow trends, and 30+ day overdue receivables/payables from the executive dashboard.
- **Bookkeeper / Accountant**: Captures supplier bills, customer invoices, petty cash receipts, and bank statements; manages the Chart of Accounts, Accruals, Fixed Assets, and General Ledger; and locks completed monthly periods.
- **Operations & HR Manager**: Manages Customer and Supplier directories, Inventory items and Stock Ledger movements, Quotes, Purchase Orders, Employee profiles, monthly UAE WPS payroll runs, and End of Service Benefit (EOSB) accruals.
- **Tax & Compliance Officer**: Generates UAE FTA VAT201 return summaries, computes UAE Corporate Tax liabilities (accounting for non-deductible add-backs, Mainland vs. Qualifying Free Zone status, and Small Business Relief), exports PEPPOL UBL 2.1 XML E-Invoices, and reviews the immutable System Audit Trail.

## Functional Requirements (Delivered Scope)

| Functional Area | Requirement Summary | Acceptance Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subscription Plans, Onboarding & Admin Console** | Provide self-service account registration with salted **PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations)** password hashing, **1 Month Free** promotional onboarding across all tiers, multi-company profile switching, tiered plan access (**Basic** AED 50/mo, **Advance** AED 75/mo, **Premium** AED 100/mo — **1 Month Free** & **Corporate Tax Only (0% VAT)**), bilingual **English / Arabic (`EN` / `عربي`)** RTL support, and an Owner Admin Console. | Users can sign up with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations) credential security, claim the **1 Month Free** promotion across Basic (AED 50/mo), Advance (AED 75/mo), and Premium (AED 100/mo), switch between company profiles within their plan limit (`1`, `3`, or `Unlimited`), toggle English/Arabic RTL layout, and request plan upgrades; the Owner Admin Console manages tenant accounts, usage metrics, and upgrade approvals. |
| **Executive Dashboard, Corporate Tax Tracker & UAE Compliance Alerts** | Display hero KPIs (Cash Runway, Burn Rate, Net Profit Margin, Current Cash), 6-month Cash Flow bar chart, overdue AR/AP hit lists, a **Live 9% UAE Corporate Tax & AED 3M Small Business Relief (Art. 21) Tracker**, a **UAE Compliance & Renewal Reminder** widget (Trade License, Establishment Card, Employee Residence Visa, and Emirates ID with 30-day urgent / 60-day warning alerts), and detailed accounting KPIs with period filtering. | Selecting a Year/Month updates P&L metrics (net of Tax Credit Notes and excluding Output VAT), refreshes the AED 375,000 0% Corporate Tax threshold progress bar and AED 3,000,000 Small Business Relief headroom, and highlights expired or expiring company and employee compliance documents. |
| **AI Document Capture & Camera Receipt TRN OCR** | Extract structured fields and line items from multi-page PDF and image Supplier Bills, Customer Invoices, Direct Expense Receipts (including mobile camera snap with **15-digit UAE Vendor TRN** & **5% VAT** OCR extraction), Petty Cash Receipts, and Bank Statements, with automatic foreign-currency-to-AED normalization. | Raw PDFs and images are submitted to the AI extraction service; camera receipts extract and validate 15-digit UAE Vendor TRNs (`100...`) and Emirate place of supply; extracted line items map to Chart of Accounts codes and semantic categories (`OpEx`, `COGS`, `Capitalized R&D`), and foreign-currency invoices are converted to AED automatically. |
| **Commercial Workflows, WhatsApp Sharing, Print Layout, Credit Notes & Direct Expenses** | Support end-to-end Quote-to-Invoice and Purchase-Order-to-Bill (3-Way Match) lifecycles, Customer PINT-AE UBL 2.1 XML E-Invoicing (Invoice `380` & Credit Note `381`), **1-Tap Single-Language (`EN` or `AR`) WhatsApp Invoice & Payment Link Sharing** (`+971` mobile normalization), an **Executive A4 Tax Invoice Print Layout** (`Dirhams Only.` amount in words, 4-field metadata card, computer-generated footer), Tax Credit Notes (`CRN-####`), Direct Expenses, and Cheque Registry tracking. | Converting a Quote creates a Sales Invoice; receiving and matching a PO creates a Supplier Bill; Sales Invoices can be previewed/printed in isolated A4 English or Arabic format and shared via WhatsApp with embedded payment links; issuing a Tax Credit Note reverses revenue, Output VAT, and open invoice receivables; Direct Expenses record immediate disbursements from Cash, Bank, or Petty Cash. |
| **Double-Entry Accounting & Deferred Revenue** | Provide a configurable Chart of Accounts (including `2150 Deferred Revenue`), manual Journal Entries with posting preview, General Ledger filtering/editing, payment-to-invoice matching, Statement of Account (SOA), and Close Month period locking. | Closing a month locks all entries on or before the lock date against deletion or backdated posting; SOA displays Total Invoice Value, Total Payments, and Running Balance. |
| **Automated & Usage-Based Schedules** | Synthesize monthly depreciation entries for Fixed Assets, pro-rated straight-line or usage-based recognition entries for Accruals, Prepayments, and Deferred Revenue (`2150`), and scheduled Recurring Transactions. | Active assets and straight-line accruals automatically generate monthly journal vouchers up to the current date, while usage-based Deferred Revenue schedules recognize revenue as delivered units/milestones are recorded. |
| **Inventory & COGS** | Maintain an Inventory Directory with bidirectional Cost/Markup/Price calculation and a chronological Stock Ledger. | Saving a new item with initial stock logs a single `IN` movement; invoicing a product logs an `OUT` movement and posts a `5000 Cost of Goods Sold` / `1300 Inventory` journal entry. |
| **Payroll, MOHRE UAE WPS & Art. 51 Statutory Gratuity** | Manage Employee salary structures and Visa/Emirates ID expiry dates, filter timesheets by employee (`#filterTimesheetEmployee` listing all active employees even before timesheets are saved) and period, process monthly timesheets, post multi-legged payroll and EOSB/leave journals (`6500 Payroll / Salaries`, `6520 EOSB & Leave Expense`), simulate and accrue **UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 Art. 51 End-of-Service Gratuity**, render and print professional branded employee payslips (with Company Logo, Address, Contact Number `#companyPhone`, Employee Designation, Starting Date, Earnings/Deductions breakdown, and Net Salary in words), and preview/export MOHRE/CBUAE `.sif` WPS files. | Saving payroll posts Gross Pay, Net Pay, Loan/Other Deductions, and EOSB/Leave accruals to P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Cash Flow, and Dashboard KPIs using the statutory tiered formula (`21 days/year` basic salary for years 1–5, `30 days/year` beyond 5 years, unpaid leave deduction per Art. 51(4), and 2-year total wage cap per Art. 51(2)), renders professional payslips in `#invoiceModal` and print view, and generates a 10-field `EDR` + 10-field `SCR` control record `WPS_<YYYY-MM>.sif` file. |
| **UAE Tax (VAT 201 & FAF), FP&A & Reporting** | Render Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Statement of Equity, Emirate-level FTA VAT 201 (Boxes `1a`–`1g` through `14`), FTA Audit File (`.csv`), UAE Corporate Tax (9% over AED 375,000), Monthly Budgeting vs. Actuals, and 90-Day Cash Flow Forecast. | VAT 201 breaks down standard-rated supplies across all 7 UAE Emirates, nets Tax Credit Notes, and exports a balanced FTA Audit File (`CompInfo`, `Customer`, `PurcData`, `SuppData`, `GLData`); Corporate Tax adds back flagged non-deductible expenses across all expense classes. |
