# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Lumen Accounting Suite

## Purpose & Product Scope

Lumen Accounting Suite provides UAE small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with a unified, browser-accessible bookkeeping, commercial operations, payroll, and tax compliance application built on **Official React 18 (`18.3.1`)**, **TypeScript** (`types/accounting.ts`, `.ts` tax/compliance engines, `.tsx` views and controllers, `renderSlot` declarative slot rendering), and **Vite** (`vite.config.ts`). This PRD defines the current short-term product deliverable scope separately from the target architecture delta documented in the companion Software Design Document (SDD).

---

## Target Personas

- **Chief Executive Officer / Founder**: Monitors Cash Runway, 90-day Monthly Burn, Net Profit Margin, 6-month Cash Flow trends, and 30+ day overdue receivables/payables from the executive dashboard.
- **Bookkeeper / Accountant**: Captures supplier bills, customer invoices, petty cash receipts, and bank statements; manages the Chart of Accounts, Accruals, Fixed Assets, and General Ledger; and locks completed monthly periods.
- **Operations & HR Manager**: Manages Customer and Supplier directories, Inventory items and Stock Ledger movements, Quotes, Purchase Orders, Employee profiles, monthly UAE WPS payroll runs, and End of Service Benefit (EOSB) accruals.
- **Tax & Compliance Officer**: Generates UAE FTA VAT201 return summaries, computes UAE Corporate Tax liabilities (accounting for non-deductible add-backs, Mainland vs. Qualifying Free Zone vs. Non-Qualifying Free Zone status, and Small Business Relief), exports PEPPOL UBL 2.1 XML E-Invoices, and reviews the immutable System Audit Trail.
