## 2. Resolved Baseline Architecture & Next-Phase Cloud Migration Roadmap

All former client-side defects and Nginx `sub_filter` workarounds have been resolved directly in the `bookkeeping-tool` source code:

### 2.1 Resolved Baseline Subsystems (Zero Runtime Monkey-Patching)
1. **Native VAT Treatment & Tax Credit Note Reversals (`script.js`, `credit-notes.js`, `vat201.js`)**:
   - Customer Invoices persist `vatTreatment` (`'standard' | 'zero' | 'exempt'`) and `placeOfSupply` (Emirate), and `credit-notes.js` issues FTA Article 60 Tax Credit Notes (`CRN-####`) that reduce `4xxx` Revenue, `2500 Output VAT`, `1200 Accounts Receivable`, and the linked invoice balance while populating VAT 201 Boxes `1a`–`1g` and FAF `SuppData`/`GLData`.
2. **Dedicated Direct Expenses Module (`index.html`, `expenses.js`)**:
   - `#view-expense` provides AI receipt extraction (`#expAnalyzeBtn`), instant disbursement account selection (`1000` Cash, `1005` Bank, `1010` Petty Cash, `2000` Accounts Payable), VAT recovery, and Corporate Tax Add-Back flagging (`source: 'Expenses'` in `bookkeeping_history_v2`).
3. **React 18 SPA Shell, Query Cache, Bidirectional URL Routing, Plan Gating & Shared UI-Kit (`react-runtime.js`, `react-store.js`, `views-dashboard.js`, `react-app.js`, `app-core-and-router.js`, `script.js`, `plan-gating.js`, `ui-feedback.js`, `ui-forms.js`, `ui-tables.js`, `ui-layout.js`, `style.css`, `components.css`, `theme-pro.css`, `ui-kit.css`, `welcome.css`, `admin.css`)**:
   - `react-runtime.js` provides a React 18 Virtual DOM reconciler (`createRoot`, `mountDeclarativeRoot`, hooks, and `__REACT_DEVTOOLS_GLOBAL_HOOK__` Fiber tree bridge across `/`, `/welcome`, and `/admin`), `react-store.js` provides tenant-scoped `LumenQueryKeys` / `LumenQueryCache` (with bounded 5xx retry and optimistic mutation rollback) and `LumenReactStore` (`useLumenStore`), `views-dashboard.js` renders `<LumenApp />`, `<SidebarNav />`, `<Topbar />`, `<DashboardView />`, and `<NotFoundView />`, and `react-app.js` loads `LUMEN_MODULE_MANIFEST` in deterministic dependency order.
   - `switchView()` and `LumenReactStore.navigateToView()` validate `.app-view` targets, render `<NotFoundView />` (`#reactNotFoundView`) on unknown routes without overwriting the requested hash, enforce `LumenPlanGate.canOpen(targetId)` on both click and inbound URL hash navigation, render semantic `18x18` SVG icons (`<SidebarNav />` in `views-dashboard.js`, superseding legacy `data-abbr` two-letter monograms) and tooltips across all 31 submenu items for collapsed-sidebar (`body.sidebar-collapsed`) navigation, invoke `renderAuditLog()` at the top level for `view-audit`, expose `Statement of Equity` (`view-equity-statement`) in the `REPORTS` sidebar menu, and synchronize `window.location.hash` and `?year=&month=` query parameters bidirectionally (`replaceState` + `hashchange`), styled with a unified Enterprise Finance Navy (`#0c1a2e`) & Royal Sapphire (`#1d4ed8`) token hierarchy and inline vector iconography.
   - `ui-feedback.js` (`LumenUI.toast`, `LumenUI.confirm`, `LumenUI.prompt`), `ui-forms.js` (inline `aria-invalid` / `.field-error` constraint validation plus `enhanceDateInputs` / `syncDateInputDisplay` `dd/mm/yy` `data-date-display` overlays on `<input type="date">` elements paired with `formatDateDdMmYy` and `formatDateTimeDdMmYy` in `profiles-storage.js`), `ui-tables.js` (`tbody[data-paginate="10"]` component-level pagination, live search, and new-row `.row-highlight`), and `ui-layout.js` (`<960px` off-canvas navigation drawer with backdrop and `Escape` dismissal) eliminate all native browser `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialogs and prevent full-page scrolling across all 20 list tables.
4. **Inventory Single-Source Quantity Tracking, Quote/PO Normalization, Ledger/Timesheet Deletion & Payroll Ledger Backfill (`script.js`, `app-ledger-actions.js`, `app-settings-and-kpis.js`, `ar-posting-fix.js`, `po-preview.js`, `po-receiving.js`, `quote-preview.js`)**:
   - `saveInventoryItem()` initializes `itemPayload.qty = 0` before calling `logStockMovement()` so initial stock is never double-counted; Quote and PO modules normalize `price ?? sellingPrice`, `cost ?? costPrice`, `client || clientName`, `supplier || supplierName`, and escape all DOM-interpolated fields against XSS; `deleteEntry()` and `deleteTimesheet()` enforce dynamic closed-period checks (`isLocked`), normalize string/numeric ID lookups, cascade-delete paired Expense + Input VAT lines or monthly payroll/EOSB entries with audit trail logging; and `ar-posting-fix.js` synthesizes collision-free persistent IDs for legacy entries, promotes unposted sales invoices and payroll journals (`isUnpostedPayrollJournal`) to `status: 'posted'`, and backfills missing ledger entries from saved `payroll_<YYYY-MM>` records only when `ledgerPosted !== true` (`backfillSavedPayrolls`).
5. **Online-Only Server/Edge Attachment Persistence, Trial Billing, 3x Lockout, Permanent Account/Profile Deletion & Multi-Tenant RBAC (`attachments-store.js`, `profiles-storage.js`, `state_repository.py`, `functions/api/[[path]].js`, `auth.js`, `welcome.js`, `admin.js`)**:
   - `attachments-store.js` offloads `fileBase64` blobs from `bookkeeping_history_v2` to `/api/v1/attachments` (SQLite `attachments` table locally and Cloudflare R2 `env.LUMEN_ATTACHMENTS` at the edge) and disables/purges local browser IndexedDB (`lumen_books_attachments`) in HTTP/HTTPS mode, preventing local data residue and enabling cross-computer admin/advisor preview, while `profiles-storage.js`, `state_repository.py`, and `functions/api/[[path]].js` enforce tenant key isolation (`X-Lumen-Account` + HMAC `X-Lumen-Session`), 3-attempt password lockout, CSPRNG 6-digit password reset (`/api/v1/auth/password-reset/request`, `/api/v1/auth/password-reset/confirm`), 1-month free trial tokenized card capture and auto-renewal (`/api/v1/auth/trial-reminder`, `/api/v1/auth/trial-renew`, `#btnToggleAutoRenew`), redact `passwordHash`, `salt`, `resetCodeHash`, and `lumen_admin_credentials` on `GET /api/v1/state`, purge `_deleted` accounts, profiles, `profile:<id>::*` keys, and `<id>::*` attachments on permanent deletion (`deleteAccountAdmin` and `remove`), and restrict administrative mutations (`setPlan`, `updateAccountAdmin`, `approvePlanRequest`, `deleteAccountAdmin`, `impersonateAccount`) strictly to the authenticated platform owner (`id === 'owner'`).
6. **UAE Corporate Tax Tracker, Compliance & PDC Cheque Alerts, MOHRE WPS / Art. 51 Gratuity & WhatsApp Workflows (`corp-tax-tracker.js`, `compliance-reminders.js`, `wps-gratuity.js`, `whatsapp-workflows.js`, `credit-notes.js`)**:
   - `corp-tax-tracker.js` (`CorporateTaxTracker` / `UAECorpTaxTracker`) computes live AED 375,000 0% bracket usage, 9% excess Corporate Tax liability, Art. 21 AED 3,000,000 Small Business Relief eligibility, and Qualifying Free Zone Person (0% QFZP) vs. Non-Qualifying Free Zone (`isNonQualifyingFreeZone`, standard 0% / 9% tiered regime) status on `#corpTaxTrackerCard`.
   - `compliance-reminders.js` (`UAEComplianceReminders`) monitors Trade License, Establishment Card, Employee Residence Visa, and Emirates ID expiry dates with 30-day urgent and 60-day warning badges rendered XSS-safely on `#uaeComplianceReminderCard`, and monitors pending Post-Dated Cheques (`evaluatePdc` / `renderPdcWidget`) clearing within 30 days on `#pdcReminderCard`.
   - `wps-gratuity.js` (`UAEWpsAndGratuity`) generates 10-field MOHRE/CBUAE `EDR` + 10-field `SCR` WPS `.SIF` files and computes UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 Art. 51 statutory End-of-Service Gratuity (`21 days/yr` for years 1–5, `30 days/yr` beyond 5 years, unpaid leave deduction per Art. 51(4), and 2-year total wage cap per Art. 51(2)).
   - `whatsapp-workflows.js` (`UAEWhatsAppWorkflows`) and `credit-notes.js` provide single-language (`en`/`ar`) 1-tap WhatsApp invoice & payment link sharing, `+971` phone normalization, 15-digit UAE Vendor TRN camera receipt OCR extraction, and an executive A4 print-isolated (`body.printing-invoice-modal`) Tax Invoice layout.

### 2.2 Target Cloud Migration Roadmap (Next Steps)
1. **Server-Side Multi-Tenant PostgreSQL Persistence**:
   - Replace `localStorage` and IndexedDB adapters with an authenticated REST API backed by PostgreSQL (`tenants`, `companies`, `accounts`, `journal_entries`, `journal_lines`, `attachments`) with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (`tenant_id = current_setting('app.tenant_id')`).
2. **Server-Enforced Authentication & Billing Webhooks**:
   - Migrate PBKDF2-SHA256 browser sessions (`auth.js`) and plan-upgrade approvals (`admin.js`) to server-issued HttpOnly session cookies / OAuth2 OIDC tokens and Stripe/PayTabs subscription webhooks.
3. **Accredited UAE Peppol Access Point Integration**:
   - Connect `EInvoiceAE.buildXml()` (`einvoice-ae.js`) to a UAE Ministry of Finance / Peppol PINT-AE Access Point for automated AS4 transmission and invoice clearance status tracking.
